🚫 1. Zero Prompt & Completion Logging Policy

CostImplodeAI does not log, inspect, store, or train on your prompt payloads or model responses.

All inference traffic passing through https://api.costimplodeai.com/v1 is processed ephemerally in volatile container RAM on Google Cloud Run. Once the response stream completes, the memory buffer is cleared. No prompt content or completions are committed to persistent disk or analytics logs.

🔑 2. CometAPI.com BYOK Key Sovereignty

CostImplodeAI is a 100% Bring Your Own Key gateway with CometAPI.com as the sole upstream AI supplier:

  • You supply your own funded CometAPI.com secret key (sk-...).
  • Keys entered in the Vault are encrypted inside your browser using PBKDF2-SHA256 + Web Crypto AES-GCM-256 before transmission.
  • Alternatively, you may inject keys statelessly per request using x-byok-cometapi-key with zero server persistence.

🪪 3. Whop OAuth Authentication & Checkout Billing

Account login and subscription payments are processed exclusively through Whop (OAuth 2.1 with PKCE and Whop Checkout):

  • When you sign in via Whop, we receive only your basic profile identifier, username/display name, email (if granted), and active membership tier.
  • Payment instruments, invoices, and tax details are handled directly by Whop. CostImplodeAI never collects or stores payment card numbers.

📊 4. Operational Metadata We Collect

  • Gateway Key Identifiers: Label, environment tag (live/test), masked token prefix (e.g. cia_live_••••••••a9e1), and RPM/spend-cap configuration.
  • Aggregated Usage Counters: Request count, token volume, live RPM, and calculated dollar savings.
  • Support Communications: Email messages sent to support@costimplodeai.com.

🤝 5. Upstream AI Supplier

When you execute live inference through CostImplodeAI, requests dispatch directly to CometAPI.com (cometapi.com) under your own BYOK credentials and credit balance.

⚖️ 6. GDPR, CCPA & International Compliance

  • You may request export or deletion of your gateway key metadata and sealed vault records at any time.
  • We never sell or rent developer credentials, personal data, or prompt traffic.
  • Contact support@costimplodeai.com for privacy requests.
